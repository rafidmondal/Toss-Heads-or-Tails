Privacy Policy

Last update: This month

Your privacy matters when using Heads or Tails at toss-heads-or-tails.raxzen.in. This page explains what data stays on your device.

1. Local Browser Storage

We do not require accounts, logins, or personal details. Your flip statistics, streak history, selected coin skin, theme, sound preferences, and virtual wallet balance are stored locally inside your own browser (localStorage) and are never uploaded to our servers.

2. External Links

Links to external platforms open in a new tab with rel="noopener noreferrer" for your security.

3. Contact

If you have any privacy or security questions, please email radidmondal@gmail.com or message t.me/rafidmondal.
