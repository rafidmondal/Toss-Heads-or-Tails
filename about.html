About Heads or Tails Online Coin Toss

Heads or Tails (toss-heads-or-tails.raxzen.in) is a lightweight, zero-download 3D coin flip web app created by Raxzen. It is designed to give anyone an instant, fair 50/50 coin toss right in the browser.

Fair 50/50 Coin Flip Design

Every toss is computed independently with equal probability for Heads and Tails. Whether you are settling a cricket toss, choosing between two options, or playing a Best-of-7 Duel with a friend, past outcomes never influence future flips.

Key Features Built In

  • Smooth 3D coin rotation with optional sound effects, haptic vibration, and particle bursts.
  • Four visual coin skins: Neon, Classic Gold, Glass, and Indian Rupee (₹).
  • Local streak tracking, win-rate statistics, and last 20 flips history.
  • Risk-free Virtual Betting mode with a resettable 1,000-coin wallet.
