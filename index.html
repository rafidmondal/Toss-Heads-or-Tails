Frequently Asked Questions

Is this Heads or Tails coin toss truly random?

Yes. Every flip generates an unbiased 50/50 random outcome between Heads and Tails, giving each side an equal 50% probability just like a real physical coin toss.

Can I use this online toss for cricket matches or kata chapa?

Yes! Many players use it as an instant online cricket toss, head ya tail call, or kata chapa toss. You can even switch to the Indian Rupee coin skin inside Settings.

How does 2-Player Duel mode work?

In Duel mode, Player 1 is Heads and Player 2 is Tails. Choose Best of 3, Best of 5, or Best of 7 rounds and flip the coin to crown the match winner.

What is Virtual Betting mode?

Virtual Betting lets you play risk-free with 1,000 free virtual coins. Pick Heads or Tails, set your bet, or try Double or Nothing—no real money is ever involved.

Is Heads or Tails free to play without downloading an app?

Yes, it is 100% free in any mobile or desktop browser with zero downloads, and you can also install it as a lightweight PWA web app.

Does the coin flipper save my stats and streaks?

Yes, your total tosses, Heads percentage, Tails percentage, current streak, and last 20 results are saved locally in your browser.
